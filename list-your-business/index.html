List your venue

Have a tap or water cooler where anyone can fill a bottle for free, with no purchase needed? List it yourself and it will appear on SipSpot as a partner refill point. There is no account, no password and no payment at this stage. Find it. Fill it. Go.

Shops, cafés, offices, councils, shopping centres, stations and chains are all welcome. Your listing shows your venue’s name, address, hours and water type under a simple “Partner refill point” label. We never show the name of the company that supplies or services your tap or cooler.

Looking after lots of sites, for example as a council, water company, shopping centre or station landlord, or a chain? Email hello@sipspot.co.uk and we can add your locations together.

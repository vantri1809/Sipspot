Terms of use

SipSpot is an information service run by J Skelton, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. It helps you find drinking-water and bottle-fill points that have been mapped as free to use. If you have a question, email hello@sipspot.co.uk.

The map is a starting point, not a guarantee. Always check a refill point yourself before you rely on it. A tap may be out of order, closed, or no longer there.

Points come mainly from OpenStreetMap, which is updated by volunteers, and may also include partner refill points that venues list themselves, which we keep separately. Hours, access and water type can be wrong or out of date. We do not inspect every point, and we give no warranty that a point will be available, safe or suitable.

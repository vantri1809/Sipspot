    Frequently asked questions

    Are these points free?

    Yes. Free for anyone, no purchase needed. Points tagged as charging a fee, or as customers-only, private or no public access, are not listed.

    When is a point open?

    Hours are shown on every point. If they are missing we say “Hours not listed”. The Open now filter only includes points whose hours we can parse and that are open at the moment.

    Where does the data come from?

    Public fountains, taps and café fill points come from OpenStreetMap. Cooler partner businesses are a separate list and are never merged with OSM data.

    What if a point is wrong?

    Use “Is this wrong? Fix on OpenStreetMap” in the popup, or contact us about a partner listing.

Privacy

Find it. Fill it. Go. SipSpot is a static website. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for a password.

Location

If you use “Find water near me”, your browser may share your location with this page so the map can sort points by an approximate walking distance. That location is used only in your browser. It is not sent to our server (the site is static files) and it is not used to build the map data.

Map data

Public fountain and café fill-point data comes from OpenStreetMap and is licensed under the ODbL. Partner business listings are a separate file and are not combined with OSM data. SipSpot lists only free walk-in fills: OSM points tagged fee=yes or access=customers, access=private or access=no are excluded, and partner listings must have walk_in_free=yes.

Advertising and cookies

A cookie consent banner must be added before ads go live. The advertisement slots on this site are placeholders only. Once Google AdSense or a similar network is connected, a consent banner is required before non-essential cookies or advertising identifiers are set, in line with UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

Affiliate links

The “Recommended reusable bottles” box is a placeholder. If affiliate links are added later, this page will say so and name the programme.

List your business

The business listing form sends the details you type, such as a name, email address and address, by email to hello@sipspot.uk or to a form endpoint configured in the site settings. Do not send passwords or payment details. We will use the information only to follow up about a listing.

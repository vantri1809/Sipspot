Privacy

SipSpot is a free map of drinking-water and bottle-fill points in the UK. You do not need an account, and we do not ask you to sign in.

Who runs this site

SipSpot is run by J Skelton, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. If you have a question about this site or about your information, email hello@sipspot.co.uk.

What we collect

The site is a set of static files. We do not run a user database, and we have not installed any analytics.

If you use the “List your venue” form, it opens your own email app with the details you typed and addresses the message to hello@sipspot.co.uk. We only see that information if you send the email. Please do not include a password or payment details. We will use what you send only to follow up about a listing.

We do not set cookies. Taps on “I filled up here”, “Still here” and “Gone” are counted anonymously against that map point, so we can show totals such as “12 refills logged by SipSpot users”. We do not ask who you are. To stop spam, our counter briefly uses your IP address in hashed (scrambled) form to limit repeat taps. The IP address itself is not stored, and the hashed key is deleted within about a day. Your browser also keeps a note of your taps in local storage (not a cookie), so the same point does not ask again on this device.

If you tap “Not working” and choose a reason, we store only which map point it is, the reason you picked and the time. The report is anonymous: we do not store your IP address or anything that identifies you, and the same short-lived hashed key described above is used to stop repeat reports. Reports are shown on the map for up to 7 days, may be passed on to whoever looks after that refill point so it can be fixed, and are deleted after a year.

Some refill units are looked after by the venue or organisation that owns them. Their staff check the unit by scanning a QR code on it. For each check we store only which unit it was, whether staff found it fine or found a problem, and the time. We keep this record for two years so the owner can show the unit was looked after. We hold the owner’s business name and an email address for alerts about their units. None of this involves visitors’ information.

If you dismiss the “Install app” tip, that choice is also stored in your browser’s local storage, so we do not show it again on this device.

When you tap Directions, your browser notes which point you were heading to in local storage for up to 4 hours, so SipSpot can ask “Did you fill up?” when you come back. This reminder stays on your device and nothing is sent to us unless you tap “Yes, log it”.

Location

If you tap “Find water near me”, your browser may share your location with this page so the map can centre on you and estimate a walking distance. That location stays in your browser. It is not sent to us.

Map tiles, directions and other services

The map background tiles are loaded from CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com). CARTO sees your IP address, as any website host would.

Fonts and map scripts are served from this site, not from third-party services.

The Directions buttons open Google Maps or Apple Maps, which have their own privacy policies. We do not receive the route you follow.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The sipspot.co.uk domain is set up with Cloudflare DNS. The tap counter runs on Cloudflare Workers.

Map data

Public fountain, tap and café fill-point data comes from OpenStreetMap and is licensed under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0). © OpenStreetMap contributors — www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Partner listings, when we add any, are a separate file and are not combined with the OpenStreetMap data.

Your rights

Because we are in the United Kingdom, UK GDPR applies to personal information we hold — for example an email you send us. You can ask to see that information, have it corrected or deleted, or ask us to restrict or object to how we use it. To do that, email hello@sipspot.co.uk.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
